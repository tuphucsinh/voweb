# VOrigin Website — Bilingual Copy Audit

Version reviewed: **1.0.0-rc5**
Scope: all generated VI/EN public pages

## Result

**PASS — bilingual editorial pass completed.**

The copy has been rewritten around four priorities:
1. premium through restraint rather than luxury adjectives;
2. trust through specificity and source-gated claims;
3. emotion through quiet, human phrasing rather than slogans;
4. English and Vietnamese authored independently rather than translated mechanically.

## Key improvements

- Reworked the English homepage, About, Brands, MARIGOLD, Capabilities, Partners, Insights and Contact copy.
- Reworked the Vietnamese equivalents for natural business Vietnamese and warmer phrasing.
- Standardised public English to International/British usage: **flavours, localisation, labelling, enquiry**.
- Replaced generic/corporate wording with more concrete language around provenance, standards, market fit and long-term growth.
- Reduced repetitive use of marketing adjectives and “curated” language.
- Tightened VOrigin master-brand language so MARIGOLD remains the first featured brand, not the identity of VOrigin itself.
- Kept portfolio-direction cards explicit that they are not signed brand partnerships.
- Kept product claims source-gated; no health, nutrition, preservative, certification or exclusivity claims are inferred.
- Improved contact copy to sound like a real B2B conversation rather than a lead-generation template.
- Localised legal routes and footer terminology for both languages.

## Automated checks

- 32 generated HTML pages: PASS
- Static QA: PASS
- Internal link/fragment check: 735 links checked, 0 errors
- English language leakage: only expected Vietnamese legal/proper names remain (company legal name and “Khởi Nguyên” in the brand story)
- AI-cliché scan: no occurrences of the blocked filler set in generated VI/EN pages
- Equivalent VI/EN routes exist for home, About, Brands, MARIGOLD, Capabilities, Partners, Insights, Contact, Privacy, Terms, and all four MARIGOLD product pages
