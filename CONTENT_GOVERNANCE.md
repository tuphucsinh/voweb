# VOrigin Content Governance

## Rule 1 — facts before copy
All product claims live in `content/claims.json`. A Vietnam-market claim must be both supported by a source and explicitly `approved_vn=true` before publication.

## Rule 2 — AI can draft, never publish
AI output is always draft-only. It may only receive approved facts plus brand voice instructions. If evidence is missing, output `[NEEDS VERIFIED SOURCE]`.

## Rule 3 — portfolio authenticity
Only signed/authorized brands may appear as brands. Future growth areas must be labelled as portfolio directions/categories, never represented with real third-party logos.

## Rule 4 — bilingual parity
Vietnamese and English pages are separate URLs. A content release is incomplete if one locale materially contradicts the other.

## Rule 5 — editorial workflow
Recommended Directus states: `draft → review → published`. Editors draft; reviewers approve; admins manage permissions/infrastructure.

## Rule 6 — source assets
Before production, replace prototype crops with official VOrigin and MARIGOLD assets supplied/authorized for use.
