## Publishing rule

VOrigin publishes MARIGOLD claims only when they are supported by either:
1. an official MARIGOLD / Malaysia Dairy Industries source; or
2. VOrigin's own Vietnam distribution documentation.

Do not infer therapeutic, disease-prevention, immunity, digestive, cardiovascular or other health outcomes unless the exact permitted wording is supported by the approved Vietnam label/dossier and the applicable regulatory basis.
